AR Headtracker

Turn your Android phone into a 6DOF head tracker for OpenTrack.

What it does

  • Inside-out 6DOF head tracking using your phone's camera and motion sensors.
  • Streams position and rotation to OpenTrack on your PC over your local network.
  • No external camera or beacons needed, the phone tracks itself in space.

Privacy

Camera images never leave your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
